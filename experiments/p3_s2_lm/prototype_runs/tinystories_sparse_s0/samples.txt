Once upon a time, there was a little girl named Lily. She loved to play with her toys all day long. One day, she found a toy she really wanted. It was a pretty dress with a star on it. Lily thought it was so pretty.

But then, her mommy came in and told her to stop playing. Lily didn't want to stop playing, so she ran away. On the way home, she saw something shiny on the floor. It was a shiny silver coin! Lily was so happy and proud of herself.

Lily learned that sometimes, things happen and it's okay to complain. She learned that even if they seem scary, they can still be real. From that day on, Lily always looked out for shiny things on her clothes.

Lily said, "Okay, I will help you." They cleaned up the toys and put them back in the big box. Soon, the toys were clean and tidy, and they said, "Thank you, Mom!" Lily smiled and said, "You're welcome, Mom. I love you."
