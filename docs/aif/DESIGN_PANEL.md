## propose:biologist

**Compartments, not scalars: a circuit-level revision of FlyAI**

The current FlyAI (flyai/model.py, block.py, memory.py, attractor.py, primitives.py) is a global-scalar caricature of circuits that are compartment-local: one pooled divisive normaliser per population, one scalar precision per level, one arousal scalar, H memory heads that each own a private KC code, and a ring whose amplitude is labelled 'certainty' but is set by an unconstrained 16-d drive. Two of these are worse than decorative: the arousal gamma_t multiplies the whole free energy and therefore cancels exactly in the Newton-preconditioned inference step (s1_new = s1 - eta*gamma*df/(gamma*(pi1+pi3)+1e-3)) and in the scale-free adaptive stop, so AIF-H3 as written compares two identical models; and the level-2 precision tracks u_proj(s1) (a target, not an error) and enters no F term. The revision keeps the filter (predict/observe/infer/write, K steps, Lyapunov-regulated Dale core, EFE decoding) and moves the fly content to where the circuits actually put it: certainty into the code (ring amplitude = pi^3, sinusoidal 2-d drive), precision into channels and compartments (diagonal variance units, APL-like local normaliser pools, one shared KC address with H compartment readouts of log-spaced timescale), surprise into a novelty MBON on the sparse key space, teaching into an MBON->DAN loop matrix with recall-gated consolidation, and state-dependent gain onto the MBON readout and DAN gain rather than onto the sparse code. Phase-1 says the whole-graph mask (real 2.611/2.523/2.442 vs shuffle 2.600/2.521/2.437 vs ER 2.605/2.516 bits/char at N=256/512/1024; dense_N 2.456/2.422) and the measured PN->KC matrix (mb 0.983 vs mb_connectome 0.982 vs mb_shuffled 0.979 on MQAR) are inert, so the connectome contributes numbers only (fan-in 5, 5 % active, 40x expansion, 15-16 compartments, 30.8 % inhibitory, 8-column rings) and every wiring arm is dropped. Every change below carries the ablation and matched control that would show it is decorative, and all are FLOP-neutral or cheaper than the current block, so the 3:1 hybrid at 125-350 M is unaffected in cost.

**New falsifiable claims**

- Arousal as implemented is inert: on TopicSwitchStream the current `arousal` and `no_arousal` conditions differ by less than the seed SD (the preconditioned step and the scale-free stop cancel gamma). The revised placement (gamma on pi0 or on beta_h) beats no_arousal on post-switch bits/char by > 2 seed-SD with CI excluding 0; null if the learned input-driven scalar sigmoid(w.s1+c) matches it.
- Gain at the code corrupts memory, gain at the readout does not: applying gamma to the PN projection / kWTA threshold makes recall of pre-switch bindings drop after a state change (state-dependent forgetting) while gain on MBON read and DAN write preserves it; measured on non-stationary MQAR with an arousal jump at the rebind.
- Shared KC address with log-spaced compartment decay priors beats separate per-head codes at matched parameters on non-stationary MQAR (pre + post rebind accuracy) by > 2 seed-SD, and loses no more than the seed SD on plain MQAR T=128 / 16 pairs; null if separate codes match on the non-stationary task.

## propose:engineer

**FlyAI v0.1: keep the filter, solve the quadratic exactly, fix the inert arousal, chunk the memory**

Keep the architecture as specified (predictive-coding filter per token; Dale-signed random-fan-in core with divisive normalisation and homeostatic gain; sparse-expansion k-WTA mushroom-body fast weights with an inferred-precision write gate; ring-attractor slow level; EFE decoding; 3:1 hybrid layout; the AIF-H1..H5 experimental structure). Two things found by reading flyai/model.py and flyai/block.py drive the revision. (1) Arousal is inert by construction: gamma multiplies every term of F, the Newton step divides by gamma*(pi1+pi3)+1e-3, so gamma cancels in the latent update to within 1e-3/P and the adaptive-K stopping test is scale-invariant; AIF-H3 currently compares the model with itself. (2) In the GPU block the free energy is exactly quadratic in s1 (Gaussian level 0 with a linear g0, quadratic priors), so the K-step gradient loop with create_graph=True (the EBT 3.3-6.6x training-FLOP class the digest lists as a dead end) is approximating a posterior that has an elementwise closed form once g0 is diagonal: s* = (pi0 x + pi1 mu1 + pi3 mu3)/(pi0+pi1+pi3). Replacing the loop by that closed form removes the second-order backprop, the amortised network A and g0 (about 16M parameters at d=768, bringing the FlyAI hybrid from 152.3M to 136.4M vs 134.5M for the GDN twin), and turns 'inferred precision' into a crisp, zero-cost test: inferred precision-ratio gate vs a learned GRU-style gate of the same fusion. Iteration is kept only where F is non-quadratic (the categorical head in model.py, i.e. AIF-H1 at CPU scale), with two numerical fixes (pi0 fixed at 1 because the NLL is already a calibrated log-likelihood; NLL curvature bound added to the step). The remaining changes are hygiene the digest asks for: precisions as bounded ratios (softmax over levels of log-precisions, no clamps) and grouped rather than scalar; a two-timescale (volatility) write gate so the dopamine gate does what the AIF-H2 hypothesis says (the current single-window gate with ga>0 reduces writes under contradiction); a delayed key so the memory is hetero-associative like the GDN twin's causal conv; exclusion of log-parameterised magnitudes from AdamW weight decay (wd on V pushes exp(V) toward 1, a 32x growth prior that the Lyapunov regulator then fights); a Rajan-Abbott E/I balance projection; Lyapunov estimation on a sub-batch outside the token loop; bump amplitude as the level-3 precision; a novelty MBON plus closed-form Gaussian KL as the epistemic term; measured-FLOP compute matching; and a confirmatory-replication rule. For the GPU path the sequential parts are handled by LaCT-style chunked delayed writes for the memory (the current per-token scatter_add keeps T copies of a 100 MB state per layer in autograd, about 200 GB per layer at T=2048, B=32), closed-form inference (no inner loop), and a compiled static-shape per-token core whose critical path is 3-4 kernels per token per layer. ## propose:scaler

**FlyAI v1 revision: a chunk-parallel, precision-gated 3:1 hybrid with fork-only refinement and action-level EFE**

The current FlyAIBlock (flyai/block.py) is ~3x the analytic FLOPs of the GDN twin and 5-20x its wall-clock because it is strictly sequential per token: the memory key is computed from the recurrent latent, the core is a nonlinear tanh Dale RNN, and K=2 autograd steps with create_graph are run on a free energy that is quadratic in s1 (g0 is nn.Linear), i.e. on a problem whose exact posterior is a closed-form precision-weighted average in which the arousal scalar cancels. The revision keeps four active-inference ingredients that the literature says are untested at scale and that have a chunk-parallel form: (1) inferred precision as the gain of a closed-form Kalman-style fusion and as an uncertainty-driven output gate on attention/fast-weight heads (vs the query-only Gated Attention control); (2) a variance-normalised surprise gate on the mushroom-body fast weights (vs raw-surprise Titans and learned GDN gates); (3) iterative refinement only at high-entropy fork tokens on the categorical (FKL) energy of the observed token (vs random-20% and refine-everywhere controls at matched FLOPs); (4) expected free energy only for discrete actions (ask/retrieve/stop), never per token. It drops the whole-graph mask and literal PN->KC matrix (phase-1 nulls: H5 real 2.494 vs shuffle 2.496 vs ER 2.491 bpc; H1 real 0.986 vs shuffled 0.992), the nonlinear Dale core and Lyapunov JVP from the GPU path (kept in the CPU reference model), the 8x16 attractor bank (subsumed by complex-eigenvalue channels of a stable linear core, which is literally the Bayesian ring filter in natural parameters), and the per-token K-step descent. Validation is a Bayesian wind tunnel (HMM state tracking, in-context HMM parameter inference, change-point HMM) with a pre-registered KL target of 1e-3 bits/token and a precision-tracking test that asks whether the inferred precision variable correlates with the true posterior concentration, which is the only test that distinguishes precision-as-belief from precision-as-gate.

**New falsifiable claims**

- C1 (inferred fusion gain): at 50M and 125M in the 3:1 hybrid, the closed-form per-channel precision-weighted fusion (change 1) matches or beats the current K=2 gradient-descent block on validation loss while cutting block FLOPs/token by >= 2x, AND beats the learned-gate blend (z = sigmoid(Wx), GRU-like) on the change-point HMM wind tunnel by > 2 seed-SD with a paired-bootstrap CI excluding 0. If the learned gate matches on both, inferred precision is redundant with amortisation and is dropped from the scaled model.
- C2 (variance-normalised surprise gate): the precision-weighted surprise gate beats BOTH the raw-surprise (Titans-style) and the learned (GDN) gate on post-rebind recall in non-stationary MQAR embedded in text, and on BABILong qa1-qa5 at 4k-16k, by > 2 seed-SD with CI excluding 0, at a WikiText-103 perplexity cost <= 1%. Prerequisite: the chunkwise (chunk-start-error) form reproduces the exact sequential form within 0.5% val loss and per-token state error < 1e-3 at chunk 64.
- C3 (fork-only refinement): refinement at the top-20%-entropy positions improves validation loss and the recall suite more than refinement at a random 20% of positions at identical FLOPs (> 2 seed-SD), matches refine-everywhere (K=2 at all positions) within seed spread at <= 1/3 of its refinement FLOPs, and is not matched by the K=0 wider control at equal FLOPs. If random-20% matches, the effect is extra compute, not uncertainty-directed refinement.
- C4 (precision-driven output gate): the gate with the inferred-precision term beats the query-only Gated Attention control on length extrapolation (ppl at 8192 after training at 2048, and NIAH at 16k) by > 2 seed-SD with CI excluding 0 at 125M; the SSMax log(n) term alone must not account for the difference (separate arm). If not, 'precision-as-attention' reduces to Gated Attention and is reported as such.
- C5 (precision is a belief): on the HMM wind tunnel the tiny FlyAI hybrid reaches KL <= 1e-3 bits/token to the true posterior predictive on state tracking and in-context parameter inference, and its inferred precision variables correlate with the true posterior concentration with Spearman rho > 0.5 and a post-change-point lag < 8 tokens, whereas the GDN twin's learned gate values show |rho| < 0.2. KL pass with rho <= 0.5 falsifies AX3 as implemented (precision is then only a gate).
- C6 (CX subsumed by complex channels): a stable linear core with complex eigenvalue channels matches the phase-1 ring-attractor bank on compass state tracking at 4x training length within seed spread and beats a real-eigenvalue-only core by > 2 seed-SD; if the ring bank wins by > 2 seed-SD, the CX module is not subsumed.
- C7 (scalability gate): the chunk-parallel FlyAI block (changes 1-3) trains within 1.5x of the fla Gated DeltaNet wall-clock at 50M and 125M at seq 2048 with the full ingredient set; every ingredient that has to be removed to meet this gate is reported as 'not scalable in this form'.
- C8 (arousal as plasticity gain): the re-purposed global gain (change 7) lowers bits/char on the 16 tokens after a topic switch by > 2 seed-SD with CI excluding 0 relative to gamma = 1, with overall bits/char not worse; otherwise arousal is dropped.
- C9 (EFE for actions): with the information-gain-about-memory epistemic term and beta_e chosen on seed 0, the action-level EFE policy on seeds 1-2 separates ask rates on unknown vs known queries by > 0.2, beats greedy on reward by > 2 seed-SD, and is at least as good as the entropy-threshold, sample-consistency and Mirostat controls; the amortised head must agree with rollout IG (Spearman > 0.7) on held-out episodes.
- C10 (no literal-wiring effect at scale): none of the literal-wiring arms (whole-graph mask, PN->KC matrix, SBM) is run on GPU; the standing prediction, from phase 1 and the external nulls, is that a magnitude-preserving, spectral-radius-matched shuffle would match real wiring within seed spread on any text task, and the repository's only remaining connectome claims are numerical priors (fan-in, coding level, radius, inhibitory fraction).
